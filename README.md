# First24Hours

**First24Hours** is an atmospheric first-person survival horror game currently in development using **Unreal Engine 5.8**.

Set in a fictional post-Soviet city in Eastern Europe, the game follows an ordinary student whose life changes dramatically when a catastrophic event strikes in the early hours of the morning.

The project focuses on immersive environmental storytelling, realistic urban environments, cinematic presentation, and the tension of surviving the first 24 hours of a collapsing world.

## Project Overview

The game takes place in a detailed residential district inspired by post-Soviet urban architecture.

The environment is designed to feel like a believable, lived-in neighborhood rather than a traditional game level. Its layout draws inspiration from real Eastern European residential areas, combining apartment buildings, interconnected courtyards, roads, parking spaces, pedestrian paths, and shared public areas.

The long-term goal is to create a cinematic survival experience in which the environment, atmosphere, and unfolding events play a central role.

## Current Development Progress

The project has progressed beyond its initial apartment prototype into the development of a larger, interconnected urban environment.

**Current development includes:**

- A residential district based on a custom urban layout.
- Multi-story apartment buildings inspired by Soviet-era architecture.
- Courtyard spaces and residential infrastructure.
- Road networks, pedestrian routes, and parking areas.
- Environmental composition focused on believable scale and spatial relationships.
- An expanding foundation for future gameplay, exploration, and narrative events.

The district serves as the foundation for the game's opening sequence and future survival gameplay.

## Setting and Atmosphere

**Location:** Fictional Eastern European city  
**Time period:** Autumn 2026  
**Genre:** First-Person Survival Horror  
**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5.8

First24Hours aims to capture the unsettling contrast between everyday urban life and a sudden catastrophic event.

Familiar apartment blocks, quiet courtyards, parked vehicles, and empty streets become the setting for an increasingly dangerous and unpredictable world.

The game's visual direction emphasizes realism, environmental detail, cinematic lighting, and a grounded post-Soviet atmosphere.

## Development Goals

The project's main development priorities are:

- **Environmental realism:** Create detailed and believable residential environments.
- **Immersive exploration:** Build interconnected spaces that encourage natural movement and discovery.
- **Cinematic storytelling:** Deliver narrative moments through the environment, lighting, sound, and scripted events.
- **Survival gameplay:** Develop mechanics centered on exploration, resource management, and decision-making.
- **Atmospheric horror:** Build tension through uncertainty, isolation, and environmental storytelling.
- **World development:** Gradually expand the playable district and surrounding urban areas.

Some of these features are planned and are not yet fully implemented.

## Running the Project

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open `First24Hours.uproject` using Unreal Engine 5.8.
3. Allow Unreal Engine to load the project and initialize any required assets or plugins.
4. Open the desired level through the Content Browser.
5. Click **Play** to test the environment.

The original Unreal Engine first-person template level has been preserved during development.
